Dominant trees and planting guidance

Primary species: Scots pine (Pinus sylvestris).

Common associated species: Norway spruce and birch.

For a Latvian hemiboreal site, final planting should follow soil type, drainage, frost exposure, biodiversity goals, and a local forester’s assessment. Avoid planting one species everywhere; mixed stands improve resilience.
